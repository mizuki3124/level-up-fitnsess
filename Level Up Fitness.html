Mission & vision

Mission

To empower individuals to achieve their desired physique through gamified, data-driven progression systems and specialized coaching tracks.

Vision

To redefine strength and conditioning into an engaging, community-centered RPG experience where physical transformation is as clear, customizable, and rewarding as digital progression.

History

Level Up Fitness started during finals week in an off-campus apartment. Two roommates, a competitive eSports gamer and an amateur powerlifter, realized that gaming progression and strength training shared the exact same psychological drive: grinding for stats, progressive overload, and mastering builds. Traditional gyms felt sterile, directionless, and intimidating, so they painted skill trees on their garage walls and started coaching peers.

What began as a DIY setup rapidly grew into a dedicated facility catering to students looking to upgrade their real-world avatars.

Logo dissection

Controller

The gaming half of the logo.

Sketch note: “Controller/Joystick”

  • D-pad on the left
  • Two round analog sticks
  • Button cluster on the right
  • White body with black detail

Dumbbell

The fitness half of the logo.

Sketch note: “Dumbbell to gain unique appearance”

  • Two hexagonal weight heads
  • Textured grip between them
  • Tilted behind the controller
  • Red, the brand’s strength color

LUF monogram

The brand’s initials as bold block letters.

Sketch note: “LUF”

  • L: solid red
  • U: white, tucked between the L and the F
  • F: white, with the arm reaching out
  • Grey outline joining all three letters

Wordmark

The full name, set in white.

Sketch note: “LEVEL UP FITNESS”

  • “Level Up” large and bold
  • “Fitness” smaller with wide spacing
  • Clean sans-serif letters

Tagline

The brand promise under the name.

Sketch note: “Build Your Real-life Character”

  • Small capital letters
  • Same white as the wordmark
  • Reads as the last line of the lockup

Changed from the sketch: the rising bar chart for ranks and levels was dropped, and the name “Level Up” carries the idea of progression.
